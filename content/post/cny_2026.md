# Chinese New Year 2026 🏮
<!--more-->

This year was a blast. I took a week off from work to celebrate with my girlfriend. The whole week wasn’t strictly for Chinese New Year—I used a couple of days to run errands for my parents, but that’s beside the point.

I spent a lot of time with Isis and her family and had plenty of fun with the homies. Most importantly, there was a ton of great food, **ang pows**, and fireworks.

### Reunion Dinner

![Family dinner](/img/posts/cny_2026/family_dinner.jpeg)

I probably can’t name everything correctly, but here goes:

- Steamed fish
- Mixed vegetables
- Poached chicken
- Huge prawns
- Siu yuk (roasted pork belly)

I’ve had the dish in the pot at the bottom of the picture a lot of times, but I keep forgetting the name. After some quick Googling, I’m torn between **Fat Choy Bao Yu** and **Luo Han Zhai**. It’s basically a braised medley with mushrooms, tofu skin, abalone, and black moss—delicious with fluffy white rice.

## Grateful
The beginning of our interracial relationship was pretty tough; I often felt out of place and a bit unwelcome during Chinese New Year celebrations. But over the years—through fears, anxiety, and trial and error—I’ve felt increasingly accepted. 

I owe it all to Isis for making me feel welcome and for standing up to anyone who tried to make me feel otherwise.
