# Chinese New Year 2026 🏮
<!--more-->

This year was a blast. I took a week off from work to celebrate with my girlfriend, Isis. The whole week wasn’t strictly for Chinese New Year—I used a couple of days to run errands for my parents, but that’s beside the point.

I spent a lot of time with Isis and her family and had plenty of fun with the homies. Most importantly, there was a ton of great food, **ang pows**, and fireworks.

Below are some of my favourite moments from the festivities. I’ve chosen not to embed the actual photos here—I don’t want this site to become a gallery for every AI bot and scraper to feast on, and I prefer posts like this to feel more like a personal log or diary entry.

---

### Reunion Dinner

I probably can’t name everything correctly, but here goes:

- Steamed fish
- Mixed vegetables
- Poached chicken
- Huge prawns
- Siu yuk (roasted pork belly)

I’ve had this stew, but I keep forgetting the name. After some quick Googling, I’m torn between **Fat Choy Bao Yu** and **Luo Han Zhai**. It’s basically a braised medley with mushrooms, tofu skin, abalone, and black moss—delicious with fluffy white rice.
